# Search and jump

- `Ctrl+F` / `Cmd+F` puts the cursor in the search box.
- `Enter` goes to the next match and `Shift+Enter` to the previous one. `F3` works too.
- The counter shows which match you are on, for example `2 of 7`.
- `Esc` clears the search.
- Click a heading in the outline on the left to jump to it. The Outline button hides the panel.
